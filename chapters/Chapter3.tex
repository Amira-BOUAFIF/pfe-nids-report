\chapter{Conceptual Design}
\section{Introduction}
This chapter focuses on the conceptual design of the application.
It presents the sequence diagrams, the class diagram,
and the deployment diagram.
Its objective is to provide a clear representation of the system structure
and the interactions between its different components.
