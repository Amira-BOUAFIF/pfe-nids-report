\chapter{Conceptual Design}

\section{Introduction}
This chapter focuses on the conceptual design of the application. It presents the sequence diagrams, which model the chronological interactions between system components for the most representative use cases, and the class diagram, which describes the static structure of the system through its classes, attributes, and relationships. Together, these diagrams provide a clear and formal representation of both the dynamic behavior and the structural organization of the system.

\section{Sequence Diagrams}

In this section, two sequence diagrams are presented to model the most representative interactions in the system. The first covers the << Trigger Network Analysis >> use case, which represents the core functionality of the platform. It models the full analysis pipeline: from the administrator triggering the simulation, through flow-by-flow prediction by the AI engine, to the conditional storage and alert generation based on the predicted label, including email notification handling for critical and high severity alerts. The second covers the << View Alerts List >> use case, modeling the interactions involved in loading, paginating, filtering, and managing alerts, as well as navigating to alert details and updating alert status.
